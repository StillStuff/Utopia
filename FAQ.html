FAQ

What Utopia is, how the recycling loop pays out, and what actually exists today.

Two games with a recycling system built into their mechanics. One is a life simulation, the other a city management sim, and both are framed as research projects run by scientists with questionable judgement. Recycling in the real world is what funds your progress in either.

Participating packaging carries a code. You recycle the item, scan the code with the app, and the value lands in your account as in-app currency. The idea is to attach an immediate, visible reward to an act that normally gives you nothing back.

Products from affiliated brands, which carry a Utopia code in their packaging artwork. Which products qualify changes with the quest rotation, so the list is worth checking before a shop.

Two things. Cosmetics for your avatar and your city, and real coupons from affiliated brands. The first is why you keep playing; the second is why recycling pays for itself.

Quests rotate. Some ask you to recycle a particular sponsored product, others are ordinary in-game objectives, and they pay out in items or currency. Achievements are permanent milestones — hitting one unlocks a cosmetic that is not available any other way.
