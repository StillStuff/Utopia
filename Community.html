Leaderboards and events

Weekly standings

Ranked by material returned, not money spent. Resets every Monday so a good week always counts for something.

Local drives

Time-boxed events for a city or campus, with a shared target. Everyone who contributes gets the reward if the target falls.

Seasonal cosmetics

Items that appear for one season and then leave. Earned through the quest rotation, never sold for real money.

Milestones

Achievements unlock cosmetics that cannot be bought — the only way to have one is to have done the thing.

  • First Return — scan your first recycled item. Starter
  • Streak of Ten — recycle something ten days running. Habit
  • Full Spectrum — return every material type the app tracks. Collection
  • Civic Duty — finish a city drive that hit its target. Event
