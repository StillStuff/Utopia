Affiliated Brands

Utopia turns packaging into a place players want to look. Here is how a brand fits into that.

Current partners

None yet. Utopia is a design concept and has no commercial partners at this stage. The programme below describes how affiliation is intended to work, not an offer that is live today.

What a partner gets

Codes in packaging

A scannable code laid into product packaging. When a player recycles the item and scans the code, they are paid in Utopia currency — and the packaging did the work of the advert.

Coupons in the shop

Players spend earned currency on real discounts alongside cosmetic items. A coupon that costs something to obtain gets redeemed more often than one handed out free.

Sponsored quests

The quest rotation can carry a brand objective — recycle three of a given product this week — sitting in the same list as ordinary in-game tasks.

Recycling data

Aggregate, anonymised figures on which packaging actually comes back — by product, region and material.

How affiliation works

  1. Assign codes. Each participating product line gets a code that Utopia can recognise, printed as part of the existing packaging artwork.
  2. Set the reward. The brand chooses what a scan is worth, and what the currency can be redeemed for on their side.
  3. Go into rotation. The product enters the quest pool and the shop, and players start finding it.
  4. Read the results. Return rates come back as a dashboard, per product and per region.
