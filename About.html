About Utopia

Recycling is a habit that can be difficult to form, especially when people can only recycle certain materials in specific geographical locations. While some companies have moved to more recyclable packaging, most people haven't caught on to this trend and have no real incentive to recycle. This app is a bundle of two games with a recycling system interwoven into their mechanics. These games are themed as research projects created by insane scientists to improve the world. Real-life recycling rewards players with an in-app currency that users can use to equip their avatars or purchase coupons from partnering companies. Recyclable products contain codes in-laid into their packaging that players can scan. Users can share their avatars and progress on social media platforms and use them as profile pictures in those applications. A shop providing in-game cosmetics for virtual currency and the coupons of partnering corporations is present to incentivize brand cooperation. The app provides a marketplace for partnering companies and brands to advertise their products through coupons and the quest system. The quest system allows for a rotation of objectives from recycling sponsored products to performing in-game objectives. The rewards can range from items to currency. Lastly, there is an achievement system that allows for unique items from reaching specific milestones. The app aims to provide a tangible reason to recycle, especially for teenagers and young adults.
